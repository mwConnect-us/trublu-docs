Bottom line: Silvair/TruBlu is the stronger choice for enterprise commercial buildings that require BMS integration, open-standards compliance, and verified BACnet interoperability. Casambi is stronger for hospitality, retail, arts, and buildings where ease of use for non-technical staff and rich consumer UX are the priority. Where mwConnect competes, the BTL-certified BACnet gateway, Bluetooth NLC certification, DLC NLC5 compliance, and HVAC integration are meaningful differentiators Casambi cannot match today.

11Weaknesses of Silvair / TruBlu™ vs. Casambi

Being honest about where Casambi is stronger helps the sales team prepare for objections:

  1. Ecosystem size: Casambi has 8M+ products vs. Silvair's ~1M devices. More luminaire manufacturers carry Casambi-ready products, giving specifiers more fixture choice.
  2. Commissioning UX: Casambi Pro (Windows + iPad) enables complete offline project configuration, floor plan uploads, drag-and-drop placement, and bulk configuration — all without internet.
  3. Consumer/Hospitality appeal: Casambi's animations, color palettes, gallery feature, gesture-based app, and circadian profiles are richer for design-focused applications.
  4. Pricing transparency: Casambi's free app lowers perceived cost-of-entry. Silvair's subscription model requires justification in proposals.
  5. Matter roadmap: Casambi is a CSA Matter alliance participant. Silvair's Matter positioning is unclear — relevant for residential-adjacent or smart home projects.
  6. Pre-commissioning / offsite planning: Casambi Pro allows most programming fully offline. Silvair's web portal requires internet. On job sites with poor connectivity, this is a real advantage for Casambi.
  7. DALI as a standalone wired solution: Casambi's Salvador Series enables a pure wired DALI system without any wireless component — a hybrid play Silvair doesn't match.

12Key Sales Objections & Responses

ObjectionResponse
"Casambi has way more products available." True — but for commercial buildings requiring BMS integration, the BACnet gateway quality matters more than ecosystem size. Our TruBlu gateway is BTL-certified with 224 BACnet objects. Casambi's option is a third-party European accessory without BTL certification.
"Casambi has a BACnet gateway too — the Lithernet." The Lithernet is made by a German company (Licht Manufaktur Berlin), not Casambi. It is not BTL-certified. It uses ASCII-based UDP packets — not a full BACnet device/object model with a PICS statement. Enterprise BMS integrators typically require BTL certification; ours has it, theirs doesn't.
"Casambi's app is free to download and easy to use." For small projects that don't need BMS, Casambi's UX is excellent. For commercial facilities teams running a Siemens/Johnson/Tridium BMS dashboard, BTL-certified BACnet is the standard they require.
"Casambi is an open ecosystem." Casambi uses a proprietary mesh protocol — every device must contain Casambi firmware. Our Silvair NLC platform uses a Bluetooth SIG-ratified open standard. Genuine interoperability requires an open standard, not an open brand.
"We've heard Casambi is more widely specified." Casambi is popular in hospitality and residential. For DLC-rebate-eligible commercial buildings with BMS, Silvair/TruBlu has the certifications (BTL, DLC NLC5) that Casambi lacks.
"Does TruBlu work with DALI fixtures?" Yes — we support DALI/D4i, and our BACnet gateway works alongside any DALI-based Silvair node in the same project.
"What happens if the cloud goes down?" Nothing. Our BACnet gateway is fully on-premise with no cloud dependency. The mesh runs autonomously without internet.
"Casambi has better HVAC integration." Silvair delivered the first qualified Bluetooth NLC HVAC integration profile — it's a Bluetooth SIG standard now, with occupancy-based thermostat setpoints through the same mesh as lighting. Casambi has no equivalent.

14Recommended Positioning

🏆 When TruBlu™ Wins — Lead With These Three Points

  1. BTL-certified BACnet gateway — the only BLE mesh platform with a BTL-certified, on-premise BACnet gateway with a full object model and PICS. Casambi's only BACnet option is a third-party, non-BTL-certified European accessory.
  2. Bluetooth NLC open standard — not a proprietary protocol. Backed by the Bluetooth SIG. Interoperable by design.
  3. DLC NLC5 listed products — eligible for energy rebates that Casambi products may not qualify for.

Add the HVAC story when applicable: "We are the first vendor to deliver a qualified Bluetooth NLC HVAC integration — occupancy-based thermostat setpoints through the same mesh network that controls lighting."

⚖️ When You Might Lose to Casambi

Be realistic with the team. If the project is a boutique hotel, restaurant, museum, residential development without BMS, or a specifier-led project prioritizing UX and fixture freedom over standards — Casambi is a reasonable choice and fighting it will frustrate the customer.

Consider proposing a hybrid: Silvair/TruBlu for BMS-connected zones, with honest acknowledgment of Casambi's strengths for non-BMS spaces.
